Three games, one skill

Each game trains one part of touch typing. Key Hunt drills where every key is: the lit key tells you which finger moves, without looking down. Word Rain trains typing whole words under time pressure. Ghost Race trains an even rhythm across a whole passage, against a pace car set to the speed you pick.

On your own keyboard layout

The on-screen keyboard in Key Hunt is the layout of your course, and keys are recognised by their physical position rather than by the character your system types. Word Rain and Ghost Race use the same words as the 34-lesson course.

How to get something out of it

  • Play after a lesson; five to ten minutes is plenty.
  • Pick a level where you get most words right, and drop down if you keep missing.
  • In Ghost Race, set the pace car a little below your real speed and raise it over time.
  • For a proper speed measurement use the typing test, not a game.

Common questions

Can I play against other people?

Not yet. Ghost Race is against a pace car that holds the speed you choose, or your own best. There are no other players and no leaderboard.

Where are my best scores kept?

Only in this browser on this device. No account, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I play on a phone?

Word Rain and Ghost Race work with a phone keyboard. Key Hunt needs a real keyboard, because it teaches where the keys are.

Why did a correct word not clear?

It has to match exactly before you press Space. Check for a stray capital or a missing letter.
