How the typing test works

You retype the passage shown above within the time you picked. TypingEase compares what you type against the passage character by character, then updates WPM, accuracy and the error count as you go. Everything runs in your browser: there is no account, and no keystroke leaves your computer. Your results are stored locally so the chart can show how the last few days went.

What WPM means

WPM stands for words per minute: how many words you type in a minute. It is the usual way to describe typing speed, and it is most useful when you compare it against your own earlier runs rather than against anyone else.

How WPM is calculated

The standard convention treats five characters as one word, spaces included. The formula is: characters entered, divided by five, divided by the minutes you spent typing. This test computes it live, so the number moves while you type and settles when the timer ends.

What counts as a good score

It depends on what you type, how long you type for and how much you have to go back and fix. A steady 45 WPM with few corrections beats a jumpy 60 that leaves a trail of mistakes. Rather than chase a number, use the test to check one thing: are you typing more evenly, with fewer errors, than you were last week?

How to type faster

  • Learn where each finger sits on the home row, and return to it after every reach.
  • Get accurate first. Speed follows accuracy; it never works the other way round.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen instead of checking the keyboard.
  • Practise in short sessions you will actually keep up.
  • Raise the pace only once the movement has stopped feeling deliberate.

The 34-lesson course puts these in order, from the two keys with a raised bump to full paragraphs at speed.

Why accuracy matters

Typing fast and then fixing a dozen mistakes costs more time than it saves, and it breaks the rhythm that makes typing feel easy. Accuracy is the share of characters you got right out of everything you entered, which is why it belongs next to WPM rather than under it.

Common questions

What is WPM?

The number of five-character words you type in one minute.

How do I test my typing speed?

Type the passage in the tool above. The timer starts on your first keystroke.

Can I look at the keyboard during the test?

You can, and at the start most people do. Work towards keeping your eyes on the screen, because that is the habit the speed is built on.

What is the consistency score?

It compares your speed second by second across the run. A high score means you held one pace instead of sprinting and stalling.
