Privacy Policy

Short version: your lessons and scores are saved in your own browser and never sent to us. Visit statistics are collected with Google Analytics only if you click “Allow” in the cookie banner.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Who we are

TypingEase operates typingease.site. For anything related to privacy, write to support@typingease.site.

2. What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or passwords: the service has no signup.
  • TypingEase sets no cookies of its own. Google Analytics cookies are set only if you allow them (see section 4).
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • What you type in lessons, tests and games is processed in your browser and is not sent to us.

3. What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the site uses your browser’s local storage (localStorage) on your own device. It holds your lesson progress and stars, badges, typing-test history, game best scores, your daily goal and streak, your keyboard and sound settings, and your chosen language, and your answer to the cookie banner. The site also installs a service worker that keeps copies of its own pages and files so that it loads faster and works offline.

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the “Clear history” button on the progress page, or by clearing the site data for typingease.site in your browser settings.

4. Third parties

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google’s servers. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Analytics, only with your consent. If you click “Allow” in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics (Google LLC). It sets cookies (_ga) and sends Google information about your visit: pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, and summary results such as completed lessons and typing speed (never the text you type). We use it only to see which pages and lessons are used, so we can improve them. Until you click “Allow”, no request is made to Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of any page; declining deletes these cookies. See Google’s privacy policy.

Apart from aggregated statistics in Google Analytics, we do not receive personal data from these providers.

5. Children

The service can be used by learners of any age and needs no personal data from anyone, including children. Google Analytics runs only if the person using the device, or a parent, teacher or school managing it, clicks “Allow”.

6. Your rights

We keep no personal data about you on our own servers; the data on your device is under your control as described above. Google Analytics data is pseudonymous and is kept by Google under the property’s retention settings; you can stop it at any time with “Cookie settings”. If you have a question or a request, contact us at the address above.

7. Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
