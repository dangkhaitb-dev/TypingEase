Privacy Policy

Short version: we never ask who you are, and what you type stays in your browser. Only if you allow it, we collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the site.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who we are

TypingEase operates typingease.site. For anything related to privacy, write to support@typingease.site.

2. What we collect

Only if you choose “Allow” in the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics (through Google Firebase) to collect anonymous usage statistics:

  • the pages you open, and which buttons and settings you use (identified by the button, not by anything you type);
  • results of lessons and typing tests: lesson, duration, speed, accuracy and number of errors;
  • the page language, and general technical information that Google Analytics derives on its own, such as device type, browser, screen size and approximate country or city.

This tells us which lessons work and where learners get stuck. It does not identify you to us: the site has no accounts, and Google Analytics does not store your IP address.

We never collect, with or without your permission:

  • names, email addresses or passwords: the service has no signup;
  • what you type in lessons, tests and games, which is processed in your browser and is not sent to us;
  • data for advertising: we show no ads and do not sell or share data.

3. Cookies and your choice

On your first visit a banner asks whether you allow analytics cookies.

  • Decline. No analytics is loaded and no cookies are set. The site works exactly the same.
  • Allow. Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*, which keep a random identifier for up to two years so that repeat visits can be counted.

Your choice itself is saved in your browser’s local storage, not in a cookie. You can change it at any time; if you withdraw consent, analytics stops and its cookies are deleted.

4. What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the site uses your browser’s local storage (localStorage) on your own device. It holds your lesson progress and stars, badges, typing-test history, game best scores, your daily goal and streak, your keyboard and sound settings, your chosen language and your cookie choice. The site also installs a service worker that keeps copies of its own pages and files so that it loads faster and works offline.

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the “Clear history” button on the progress page, or by clearing the site data for typingease.site in your browser settings.

5. Third parties

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google’s servers. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Analytics. Only with your consent, usage statistics are processed by Google Analytics, a service of Google LLC, which may process them on servers outside your country. See Google’s privacy policy and “How Google uses information from sites that use our services”.

We do not receive personal data from these providers, and we do not use their data for advertising.

6. Children

The service can be used by learners of any age. It asks no one, including children, for personal data, and analytics runs only after someone using the device allows it.

7. Your rights

We hold no data that tells us who you are, and the data on your device is under your control as described above. You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time with the “Cookie settings” button above; usage statistics already collected are kept by Google Analytics for a limited period, no longer than 14 months, and then deleted. If you have a question or a request, contact us at the address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
